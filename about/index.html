About

I'm Dejan Tumenko, a Creative Director based in Belgrade. My approach to design is shaped by what I do off the clock — shooting on a Canon, filling Field Notes notebooks, printing photos from every trip I take. I've always been drawn to things made with intention: the right pen, the right paper, the right frame. That obsession with craft and detail is exactly what I bring to branding, art direction, and every project I take on.
