Nordarch Studio is a Belgrade-based architecture firm with a Nordic design philosophy — where structure meets restraint. The brief called for a visual identity that communicates authority, precision, and timelessness. The resulting brand system draws from raw textures, muted tones, and tight typographic hierarchy — reflecting the studio's approach to space and form. Every touchpoint, from business cards to digital presence, was crafted to feel intentional and enduring. The identity positions Nordarch as a studio that doesn't follow trends — it defines them.
