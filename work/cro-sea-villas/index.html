Cro Sea Villas

Cro Sea Villas

2021

Overview

This art direction project distills the spirit of a premier Adriatic villa retreat into a complete visual identity. Crisp whites, deep navies, and Mediterranean light guide every design decision — from branded amenities to social presence. The result is a refined, consistent brand world that speaks directly to the luxury traveler seeking privacy, elegance, and the magic of the Croatian coast.
